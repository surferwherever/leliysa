Who I am

About me

He is a dedicated researcher in renewable energy, solar optimization, smart grids, and power electronics. He earned his Ph.D. in Electronics and Telecommunications in 2021 from the prestigious Mohammadia School of Engineering at University Mohamed V in Rabat, Morocco. His research focuses on optimizing photovoltaic systems, advancing power electronics, and integrating smart grids.

From 2017 to 2019, he broadened his expertise through research programs under Erasmus+ and Horizon Europe’s Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions at Vilnius Gediminas Technical University in Lithuania. Currently, as a Senior Analog Engineer at Mu-Electronics, a contractor for NXP Semiconductors, Dr. EL IYSAOUY contributes to over 50 impactful projects in Battery Management Systems for vehicles and IoT.

            Rules of thumb

            • Wider transistor (W): more current, so a faster edge, but a heavier load on the gate before it.
            • Longer channel (L): a weaker transistor, so a slower edge.
            • A PMOS is about 2.5 times weaker than an NMOS of the same size, so it is drawn wider.

              Experience in detail

              on behalf of
              • Layout

                End-to-end analog and mixed-signal layout in Cadence Virtuoso for critical blocks of automotive ICs: 50+ commercialized projects, delivered on schedule.

              • Full-custom flow

                Component placement, matching techniques (common centroid, interdigitation), routing, shielding and parasitic-aware layout.

              • Manufacturability

                Best trade-off between area, electrical performance and yield: device matching, routing parasitics and thermal considerations, low noise and robustness across PVT (Process, Voltage, Temperature).

              • Verification

                All DRC and LVS violations resolved across TSMC+, SOI and CMOS14 process technologies; parasitic extraction and schematic / layout correlation with circuit designers.

              • Reliability

                IR drop, electromigration, coupling noise and signal integrity handled with optimized routing, guard rings, shielding and substrate isolation; power-integrity and reliability analysis.

              • Functional safety

                Physical implementation of PMICs and SBCs, integrated with MCUs / MPUs, in line with ISO 26262 functional safety (ASIL B to D), AEC-Q100 and IEC 61508; energy efficiency and power-loss reduction.

              • Collaboration

                Layout reviews and chip-level integration with design, verification and R&D teams across 10 global R&D centers; mentoring of junior engineers.
